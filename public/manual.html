About the Application

What Kopi Rider is and how it works at a glance.

Kopi Rider is a coffee truck booking & lead generation platform built with Laravel 11. It is not an online store — there is no cart, no checkout and no automated ordering. The entire system is designed around one goal: turning website visitors into qualified event leads that flow into WhatsApp, where every booking is confirmed by a human.

Core System Principles

  • A lead must never be lost. Customer data is saved to the database first, before the WhatsApp redirect. Even if WhatsApp fails to open, the data is already safe and ready for follow-up.
  • Partial leads are still saved. A customer who fills in half the form and quits is still recorded for follow-up.
  • Human confirmation. Every booking is confirmed through a WhatsApp conversation, not by the system.

Login & Forgot Password

How to enter the admin panel and recover your account.

3.1 — Signing In

  1. Open the login pageNavigate to /login in your browser (e.g. https://yourdomain.com/login).
  2. Enter email & passwordUse a registered staff account. The eye button 👁 next to the password field lets you peek at what you typed.
  3. Click Sign inOn success you are redirected to /admin (Dashboard).

3.2 — Forgot Password

  1. Click "Forgot password?"On the login page, click the link below the form.
  2. Enter your emailThe system sends a reset link in a fully branded Kopi Rider e-mail.
  3. Open the e-mail & click the reset buttonThe link is signed, single-use and expires after 60 minutes.
  4. Set a new passwordThen simply sign in with the new password.
⚠️ ImportantReset links are valid for 60 minutes only. If it expires, just request a new one. Check the spam folder if the e-mail doesn't arrive.
💡 Change password while logged inAlready signed in? Change your password via Settings → 🔐 Change password (see chapter 12).

Dashboard

Your business at a glance — the first page after login.

The Dashboard (/admin) shows a quick snapshot of business health. It's the starting point every time you open the panel:

  • 📊 Lead statistics — how many leads came in (complete & partial) in the selected period.
  • 💰 Revenue — total confirmed payments (paid) from deposits + balances.
  • 📅 Booked days — how many days are already booked on the calendar.
  • Recent activity — the newest incoming leads so nothing gets missed.

Suggested Daily Routine

  1. Open Dashboard → check today's new leads.
  2. Open Leads → process leads with status new (see chapter 5).
  3. After a deal → set status to confirmed, mark the date booked in Calendar, and record the deposit in Payments.

Leads — Prospective Customers

The operational heart: manage every enquiry from arrival to deal (or lost).

5.1 — Leads List

Open 📝 Leads in the sidebar. The table shows every lead with its reference, name, event date, event type, package and status. Filters are available at the top:

FilterUse
Statusnew / contacted / negotiating / confirmed / lost
CompleteOnly complete leads, only partial, or all
SearchFind by name, WhatsApp number or reference (e.g. KR-0012)

5.2 — Status Workflow

Every lead moves through the pipeline below. Change the status from the lead detail page.

new→ contacted→ negotiating→ confirmed ✓  or  lost ✕
StatusMeaningYour action
newJust came in, not yet contactedOpen WhatsApp & greet the customer
contactedContacted, waiting for a replyFollow up if they go quiet
negotiatingNegotiating package/price/dateAgree on details & send the offer
confirmedDeal! Booking is finalSet the date to booked, record payments
lostCancelled / didn't happenOptionally note the reason

5.3 — Lead Detail Page

Click any lead to open its details. Here you can:

  • See the full enquiry data — date, event type, guest count, chosen package, and the generated WhatsApp message.
  • Open WhatsApp chat — a one-click button that opens a chat with the customer using a pre-written message.
  • Change the status — following the workflow above.
  • Manage Payments — record deposits & balances (see chapter 7).
  • Delete the lead — only when truly necessary (lead data is valuable, think twice).
✅ Best practiceRespond to new leads within 24 hours. Reply speed is the single biggest conversion factor for event businesses.
💡 Partial leadsPartial leads (customer quit the form midway) still appear in the list. The data already captured (e.g. date + event type) is enough for a proactive WhatsApp follow-up.

Calendar — Availability

Control which dates are open for booking on the website.

6.1 — How It Works

Open 📅 Calendar. The calendar shows each date's status. Click any date to change it — saving is instant (no save button).

StatusSymbolEffect on the website
Available🟢Customers can pick this date
Enquiry🟡Shows as "someone is asking" — customers can still enquire but know someone else asked first
Booked🔴Date is locked and cannot be picked
💡 AutomaticWhen a customer requests a date through the booking form, that date automatically turns 🟡 enquiry on the public calendar — no action needed from you.

6.2 — Per-Date Notes

Every date can carry an internal note (e.g. "PT ABC EVENT — deposit due 20 Sep"). Notes are never visible to customers, only in the admin panel.

6.3 — When to Change Status Manually

  • Lead confirmed → set the date to booked + record payments.
  • Private event / truck maintenance → set booked directly so no enquiries come in.
  • Enquiry cancelled / negotiation failed → set it back to available.

Payments & Midtrans

Record deposits & balances, send payment links, or use bank transfer.

7.1 — Concept

Payments are recorded per lead (not per package). Each lead can have up to two records:

TypeFunctionWhen to use
DepositDown payment that locks the dateAt deal time / status confirmed
BalanceRemaining settlementBefore or on the event day

Each payment has a status: pending → paid.

7.2 — Adding a Payment

  1. Open the lead detail pageLeads → click the intended lead.
  2. Scroll to the Payments sectionChoose the type (deposit/balance), enter the amount and method.
  3. SaveThe record appears with status pending.
  4. Generate a payment link (optional)For the Midtrans method, click Generate link — the system creates a payment link you can send to the customer.
  5. Mark as paid once the money arrivesChange the status to paid after confirmation (automatically via the Midtrans webhook, or manually).

7.3 — Midtrans vs Bank Transfer

Midtrans (payment link)Bank transfer (manual)
FlowLink sent to customer → pays (VA, QRIS, card, e-wallet) → webhook updates status automaticallyYou send the account number → check the mutation → mark paid manually
Best forMost customersCustomers who don't use digital methods
SetupSettings → 💳 Payments (see chapter 12.2)None
💡 Midtrans webhookPayment notifications are sent by Midtrans to /api/midtrans/notification. As long as the credentials are correct, payment status flips to paid automatically without manual input.
⚠️ Don't mark paid prematurelyFor manual bank transfers, always verify the account mutation before switching the status to paid.

Media — Hero & Gallery

  1. Open 🖼️ Media in the admin sidebar.
  2. Pick a file — hero image (main landing-page photo) and/or gallery photos.

9.2 — Managing Gallery Photos

⚠️ Photos not showing after upload?Run storage:link from the 🖥️ Terminal menu (chapter 11). This creates the public/storage symlink required for files to be visible on the web.

Analytics

The booking conversion funnel — see exactly where customers drop off.

Open 📈 Analytics. This page shows the conversion funnel built from tracking events wired into the booking flow:

Open form→ Pick date→ Pick type & guests→ View packages→ Enter contact→ Submit + WhatsApp

Events are sent automatically by the website to the /api/analytics endpoint every time a customer completes a step.

How to Read It

  • A big drop at one step → that step has a problem (too complicated, unclear pricing, etc.) — fix it before driving more traffic.
  • Many submits but WhatsApp never opens → check the message preview / WhatsApp number in Settings.
  • Use this data for content decisions: photos, starting prices, package order.

Terminal — Artisan Without SSH

Run deployment commands straight from the admin panel.

Open 🖥️ Terminal. From here you can run Laravel artisan commands without SSH access to the server — handy on shared/managed hosting.

11.1 — Quick Commands

CommandWhen to use
migrate --forceAfter an app update — apply new database migrations
storage:linkUploaded photos not visible on the web → create/recreate the storage symlink
optimizeAfter big changes — cache config, routes & views for speed
optimize:clearChanges not visible on the web → flush all caches
migrate:statusCheck which migrations have/haven't run
cache:clear / config:clearClear a specific cache
route:listList every registered URL
aboutLaravel, PHP & environment version info

11.2 — Free Input

Type any artisan command in the input box (no php artisan prefix needed) and press Enter or click Run. Command history can be recalled with ↑ / ↓. Examples:

$ migrate --force
$ config:cache
🛡️ Safety Only artisan commands are accepted. Interactive/long-running commands (tinker, serve, queue:work) are blocked, and destructive ones (migrate:fresh, db:wipe) ask for confirmation. Commands run inside the PHP process (Artisan::call()) — compatible with hostings that disable exec.

Settings

All application configuration on one page.

12.1 — 🌐 General

  • WhatsApp number — the destination number for all enquiries (international format without +, e.g. 6281234567890). Important: this number powers the booking buttons across the whole website.
  • Instagram / TikTok URL — social links in the footer & social section.
  • Hero badge text — small text on the landing-page hero badge.

12.2 — 💳 Payments (Midtrans)

  • Fill in the Server Key, Client Key and Sandbox / Production mode from your Midtrans dashboard.
  • These keys power the Generate link button on payments (chapter 7).

12.3 — 📧 E-mail (SMTP)

  • Full configuration: host, port, TLS/SSL encryption, username, password, from-address & name.
  • Credentials are stored in the database and applied at runtime — no .env editing or redeploy needed.
  • Saving with an empty password field does not wipe the old password (anti-frustration).
  • Use the 📨 Send a test e-mail button to verify — a fully branded test e-mail is sent to the From address.
💡 E-mail templateEvery e-mail (password reset, tests, future notifications) uses one template: logo header on dark brown, gold divider, cream content card, CTA button and a footer with social links. The logo is embedded inline (cid:) so it displays even when the mail client blocks remote images.

12.4 — 📄 Legal Pages

Edit the Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions content shown at /privacy-policy and /terms-and-conditions.

12.5 — 🖼️ Logo & Favicon

Upload your own logo and favicon to replace the defaults. Applies instantly across the website, admin panel and e-mails.

12.6 — 🔐 Change Password

  1. Enter your current password, then the new password twice.
  2. Save — the next login uses the new password.

12.7 — 👥 Staff Users

  • Add staff — fill in name, email, password; they can sign in immediately.
  • Change role — promote/demote a user.
  • Delete user — revoke panel access.
⚠️ Safe practiceOnly create accounts for people who actually operate the panel. Delete staff accounts as soon as they become inactive.

Complete End-to-End Workflow

From the customer's first click to the event day — with the admin's role at every stage.

  1. Customer fills in the website formDate → event type → guests → package → contact. Partial leads are auto-saved at every step.
  2. Lead arrives in the panel (status: new)The requested date automatically turns 🟡 enquiry on the public calendar.
  3. Admin opens WhatsApp & greetsFrom the lead detail: click Open WhatsApp chat. Change status → contacted.
  4. NegotiationAgree on package, final price and details. Status → negotiating.
  5. DealStatus → confirmed. Set the date to booked in Calendar (+ internal notes).
  6. DepositAdd a deposit payment on the lead detail → generate a Midtrans payment link (or send bank details). Mark paid when funds arrive.
  7. Preparation & documentationUpload event photos to Media afterwards — the gallery grows and the website gets stronger.
  8. BalanceCollect the settlement (record balance, mark paid once received).

FAQ & Troubleshooting

Common problems and their solutions.

A customer says WhatsApp didn't open after submitting — is anything lost?

Nothing is lost. The lead was saved before the redirect. Find the customer's reference number (KR-XXXX) in the Leads menu, then contact them via the WhatsApp number on record.

Uploaded photos don't appear on the website

Open Terminal → run storage:link. If it still doesn't work, run optimize:clear then refresh the page (hard refresh: Ctrl+Shift+R).

Password reset e-mails are not being sent

  1. Check Settings → 📧 SMTP — click Send a test e-mail to verify.
  2. Ask the customer to check their spam folder.
  3. SMTP changes apply at runtime — no redeploy needed.

Admin changes are not visible on the website

Cache. Open Terminal → optimize:clear. Once everything is stable, run optimize to re-enable the performance cache.

A Midtrans payment doesn't flip to paid automatically

  1. Make sure the Server Key/Client Key & mode in Settings → 💳 Payments are correct.
  2. Make sure Midtrans notifications point to https://yourdomain.com/api/midtrans/notification.
  3. In the meantime, mark paid manually after verifying the funds arrived.

A date is deal-confirmed but still green on the website

Booked status is not automatic from lead confirmation — set it manually in Calendar (click the date → booked). This gives you full control over availability.

Admin password forgotten

Use Forgot password? on the login page (chapter 3.2). If e-mail is also broken, have another staff member create/reset your account via Settings → 👥 Staff users.
